About the Project

How the glossary was developed and checked.

01 · Topic

Information Technology and Web Technologies were selected because the field has a large, internationally used vocabulary and established terminology resources in English, Kazakh and Russian.

02 · AI assistance

ChatGPT was used to structure the glossary, draft concise definitions, examples and translations, and generate the HTML/CSS/JavaScript interface.

03 · Verification

AI-generated terminology was compared with Termincom's terminology database and IT terminology dictionary. Technical definitions were cross-checked against MDN Web Docs. Entries were edited for consistency and context.
